## Method and limits

- Triage: `scripts/upstream-sync/triage.py` (exit 0, report written, last step equals the pinned head). Group changes made by reading are on the commit lines. `[rich]` marks a commit tagged rich.
- Read: `git show --stat` and diffs for every Risk, Gate off and entry-point commit and the rich-tagged commits that touch Mac twins; subjects and stats for Comes along and iOS-only.
- SDK checks: framework presence and `@available(macOS, unavailable)` marks in the macOS 27 SDK (Xcode 27.1) under `xcrun --sdk macosx --show-sdk-path`. They show what the compiler will refuse. They do not replace the build.
- Conflict forecast: `git merge-tree <base> macos upstream/main` (read-only, legacy form, no objects written). The real merges run in 5 steps, so the hunks differ in detail.
- Not done: no `xcodebuild`, no worktree, no merge, no commit. The Mac ports list is a forecast; the Phase 2 builds find the rest.
